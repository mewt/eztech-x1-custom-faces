# Where these faces came from

The GIFs in this folder are a community-shared emoji pack for XiaoZhi AI
speakers, collected and published by **Loa AI** (loaai.me):

<https://loaai.me/bai-viet/thay-icon-bieu-cam-cho-loa-ai-xiaozhi-emoji-gif-cho-robot-ai-chatbot>

That page credits the pack to the Loa AI Xiaozhi community ("Bộ tài nguyên
emoji đã được chia sẻ từ cộng đồng Loa Ai Xiaozhi") and names no individual
author. **No licence or redistribution terms are stated anywhere**, so treat
these files as all rights reserved by whoever drew them. They are included here
only so the build runs end to end without hunting for assets.

If you drew these, or you hold the rights and want them taken down, open an
issue and they will be removed the same day — the build does not depend on
them. `tools/build_faces.py` works with any folder of GIFs: point
`_source_pack` in `tools/face_map.json` at your own and nothing else changes.
